For Supported Housing staff on supported voids. Fill in the property details, tick the repairs needed in each room and add measurements where asked. Submitting creates the PDF report, saves it to SharePoint and emails it to you, the officer and the property's surveyor. You can also complete the optional inventory at the end, to record which items stay, which are removed and which need cleaning.
